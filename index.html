OUR STORY

Meet Manee, our lucky little chef.

MANEEKO is the frozen food brand of N L Industries. Inspired by the joy of sharing food, Manee brings a little playfulness and good fortune to every gathering.

Our growing range includes vegetarian, paneer and chicken momos, along with vegetarian and non-vegetarian dim sums.

Manufacturing by N L Industries

Fresh ingredients, thoughtful portions and frozen food-service solutions for India.

Convenient 1 kg Food-Service Packs

MANEEKO momos are designed with a standard portion weight of approximately 21 g per piece, offering around 47–48 momos per 1 kg pack.

Available in Veg, Paneer and Chicken variants, our frozen momos are designed for restaurants, cafés, cloud kitchens and food-service businesses seeking consistent portioning, convenient preparation and menu flexibility.

Fresh Ingredients, Every Day

Freshness is at the heart of MANEEKO. We follow a daily fresh-procurement approach, with vegetables and other fresh ingredients sourced every day for production. Our production process is designed to utilise the day's freshly procured ingredients completely, minimising wastage and avoiding the carryover of unused fresh produce into subsequent production days.

This approach reflects our commitment to ingredient freshness, responsible sourcing and consistent product quality.

Protein-Focused Recipes

Our recipes are being developed with a focus on protein content and balanced ingredients, without compromising on flavour or texture.

Detailed nutritional information, including protein content per serving, will be published following recipe validation and nutritional testing.
